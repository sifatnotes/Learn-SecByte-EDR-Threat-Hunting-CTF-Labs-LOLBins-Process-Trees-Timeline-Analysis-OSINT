## Learning Path

1. **Explore endpoint activity:** Begin with living-off-the-land concepts and command-line investigation.
2. **Trace execution:** Study process trees and related tracing techniques.
3. **Build investigative timelines:** Practice organizing evidence and triaging events.
4. **Develop EDR workflows:** Explore the three EDR-focused labs to strengthen endpoint investigation skills.
5. **Connect intelligence:** Finish with intelligence fusion, OSINT, and campaign tracking.

Adjust the sequence to match the prerequisites and learning objectives specified by each lab.

## How to Use the Labs

1. Open the official lab through its direct link.
2. Review the objectives and environment requirements.
3. Follow the instructions in the authorized training environment.
4. Record relevant events, evidence, and investigative reasoning.
5. Summarize findings and identify useful defensive or detection improvements.
